Think of it like this: when you're looking at a Ford F-150, for example, you'll see an XL, an XLT, a Lariat, and so on. Those are the trim levels. The XL is usually the workhorse – basic, gets the job done. Then you move up – the XLT might add some nicer interior touches, maybe a little more chrome, and the Lariat typically includes things like heated seats or a bigger screen. It's just a way the manufacturer offers different packages of features within the same truck model. Doesn't always mean a higher trim is *better* for you, just different.
