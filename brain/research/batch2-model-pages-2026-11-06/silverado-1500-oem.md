## SILVERADO FAQS

### What is the highest level 2027 Silverado model?

The highest level 2027 Silverado depends on what you need from your truck:

If strength, modern sophistication and evolved premium comforts are what you value most, then the High Country is the model for you.

If ultimate off-road capability and leading the pack in ruthless conditions is the versatility you demand, then the ZR2 is the right answer.

### What engines are available on the 2027 Silverado?

The 2027 Silverado offers four engine options, each providing different capabilities depending on your needs:

- 6.6L V8 Engine
  - 481 max available horsepower
  - 501 lb.-ft. max available torque
  - 1,510 lbs. max available payload\*(35)
  - 9,700 lbs. max available towing\*(36)
- 5.7L V8 Engine
  - 402 max available horsepower
  - 428 lb.-ft. max available torque
  - 2,170 lbs. max available payload\*(37)
  - 13,700 lbs. best-in class\*(38) max available towing\*(39)
- TurboMax® Engine
  - 350 max available horsepower
  - 455 lb.-ft. best-in-class\*(40) max available torque
  - 2,340 lbs. max available payload\*(41)
  - 9,400 lbs. max available towing\*(42)
- Duramax® 3.0L Turbo-Diesel Engine
  - 305 max available horsepower
  - 495 lb.-ft. max available torque
  - 1,870 lbs. max available payload\*(43)
  - 12,000 lbs. of max available towing\*(44)

### Does the 2027 Silverado offer a diesel engine?
