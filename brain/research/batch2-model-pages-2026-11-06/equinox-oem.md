The overall length of the 2027 Chevy Equinox is 183.2" (just over 15 feet).

The 2027 Chevy Equinox seats 5.

### Does the Chevy Equinox have a 3rd row?

No, it does not. The 2027 Chevy Equinox is a 2-row SUV that seats 5.

Equinox is considered either a crossover or a compact SUV.
