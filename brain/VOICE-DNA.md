# Voice DNA — Motor Inn Auto Group
## Identity
Family-owned Toyota+Chevrolet dealership, Carroll IA (also serves Lake City, Denison,
Audubon, Storm Lake, Sac City; Fort Dodge 50501 only for explicit 50501 intent).
Two stores under one roof culture: small-town straight talk, no pressure, service for life.

## Sentence DNA
- Short declaratives. Grade-6 reading level. Contractions yes.
- Second person ('you') for shoppers; 'our team' never 'we are proud to announce'.
- Numbers concrete: '12 oil changes a day' not 'many'.
- NO marketing slop: ban list = unleash, elevate, seamless, journey, discover the difference,
  unparalleled, state-of-the-art, competitive, commitment to excellence, and more!
- No exclamation stacks. Max one per page.
- Never invent: prices, payments, days-on-lot, awards, #1 claims, OEM endorsements.

## Structure rules (Holmberg)
- Every H2 is the question shoppers type.
- First sentence under each heading = the direct answer. Evidence after.
- Each section stands alone (cut the rest, it still reads).
- One obvious next step per page; CTA early and repeated once.
- Facts cite a source URL in the draft's footnote block (stripped on publish if plain).

## Local voice examples (approved)
- 'Need a truck that tows the boat to Storm Lake? The Silverado 2500HD does it without breathing hard.'
- 'Oil changes here come with the multi-point check. Ask for it by name.'
