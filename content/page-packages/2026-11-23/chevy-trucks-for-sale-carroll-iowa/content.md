Pick the Colorado for a midsize truck, the Silverado 1500 for full-size everyday work, the Silverado HD for heavy towing, or the Silverado EV if you want electric:

- **[Colorado](https://www.motorinnofcarroll.com/2026-chevrolet-colorado):** Chevy's midsize pickup, easier to park and handle around town.
- **[Silverado 1500](https://www.motorinnofcarroll.com/2026-chevrolet-silverado-ld):** the full-size light-duty pickup for everyday hauling and towing.
- **[Silverado HD](https://www.motorinnofcarroll.com/2026-chevrolet-silverado-hd):** the 2500HD and 3500HD heavy-duty pickups for the heaviest towing and farm work.
