Used Trucks for Towing in Iowa

Shopping a used truck to pull a trailer? Start with the trailer weight, not the grille. Then match the tow rating, payload, and hitch setup to that exact truck.

A used truck is a good towing pick when the exact truck can handle your load and it shows you that with documentation. Newer Silverado 1500 models can tow 7,200 to 13,700 lb; F-150 models 8,400 to 13,500 lb; Ram 1500 up to 11,610 lb.

Can a used truck tow safely through an Iowa winter?

Yes. Iowa can bring ice and snow; inspect the trailer, hitch, safety chains, breakaway wiring, trailer brakes, and tires.

How do I check a used truck before towing?

Match the VIN's towing package and axle configuration; inspect the hitch frame, wiring, lights, controller, tires, brakes, and service history.

Should I buy a half-ton or a three-quarter-ton used truck?

A half-ton is right for a light trailer and occasional weekend trip; a three-quarter-ton suits regular towing or heavier loads.
