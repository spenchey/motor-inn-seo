# GBP Posting Strategy — 2026-07-02
Based on GBP Velocity Audit of 2026-07-02

## Recommended Cadence
- Frequency: 2 core posts per week.
- Best days: Tuesday and Thursday.
- Optional flex slot: Sunday afternoon only when a seasonal or local-use angle is truly timely.

## Mid-July Content Mix
- 40% service and seasonal utility
- 30% inventory or certified-pre-owned value
- 30% local lifestyle, buyer education, or practical FAQ

## Priority Angles
1. Post-holiday inspection and AC performance checks after July 4 travel.
2. Truck, SUV, and cargo-room practicality for Carroll, Spirit Lake, and Okoboji drivers.
3. Clear used and certified value for buyers who want straight answers instead of hype.
4. Family-use and commute-fit posts that help shoppers narrow the field quickly.
5. Topics that can also be reused on Facebook and Instagram because Google is surfacing stronger profile-freshness signals.

## Competitor Signals To Counter
- Champion Ford still has visible owner-post history in the live Google panel and uses simple vehicle and promo hooks.
- Spirit Lake Ford and Okoboji still show active updates/profile modules, which is enough to signal freshness.
- Motor Inn still has no current visible owner post in the accessible live view, so the immediate win is visible consistency.

## Location Rotation
- Carroll first
- Spirit Lake or Okoboji second
- Then one surrounding-market mention such as Denison, Lake City, Spencer, Storm Lake, or Sac City
- Do not repeat the same area in back-to-back posts

## Post Rules
- Keep copy between 100 and 150 words
- Use a real Motor Inn photo every time
- Include one target keyword and one place mention
- End with a clear CTA tied to phone or site
- Never mention days on lot
