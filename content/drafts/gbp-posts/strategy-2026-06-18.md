# GBP Posting Strategy — 2026-06-18
Based on GBP Velocity Audit of 2026-06-18

## Recommended Cadence
- Frequency: 2 core posts per week.
- Best days: Tuesday and Thursday.
- Optional flex slot: Sunday afternoon only when a holiday, lake-weekend, or specific inventory angle makes it earned.

## Late June / Early July Content Mix
- 40% service and seasonal utility
- 30% inventory or certified/pre-owned value
- 30% local lifestyle, buyer education, or trip-planning relevance

## Priority Angles
1. Road-trip readiness before Fourth of July travel volume picks up.
2. Lake-season towing, cargo room, and truck/SUV practicality for Spirit Lake and Okoboji shoppers.
3. Used and certified value for buyers who want clarity instead of surprises.
4. FAQ-style posts that answer real local questions in plain language.
5. Reuse the best GBP angle on Facebook or Instagram because Google is surfacing stronger social-freshness signals in profile experiences.

## Competitor Signals To Counter
- Spirit Lake Ford is running a four-post offer burst dated June 9 through June 30 with model-specific savings and direct CTAs.
- Champion Ford is still using the single-model inventory spotlight pattern.
- Motor Inn still had no visible current owner post in the accessible live view on June 18, 2026, so publishing rhythm matters more than complexity right now.

## Location Rotation
- Carroll first
- Spirit Lake or Okoboji second
- Then one surrounding-market mention such as Denison, Sac City, Storm Lake, Spencer, or Lake City
- Do not repeat the same area in back-to-back posts

## Post Rules
- Keep copy between 100 and 150 words
- Use a real Motor Inn photo every time
- Include one target keyword and one place mention
- End with a clear CTA tied to phone or site
- Never mention days on lot
