# GBP Posting Strategy — 2026-05-14
Based on GBP Velocity Audit of 2026-05-14

## Optimal Posting Schedule
- **Frequency:** 2 posts per week
- **Best Days:** Tuesday and Thursday
- **Optimal Time Window:** 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM (CST)
- **Rationale:** Based on competitor analysis showing peak engagement during these times for automotive service businesses in western Iowa market.

## Post Type Mix
- **Offers:** 50% (1 post per week)
- **Updates:** 30% (0.6 posts per week - rounded to 2 updates every 3 weeks)
- **Events:** 20% (0.4 posts per week - rounded to 1 event every 2-3 weeks)

## Topic Rotation Schedule
Cycle through these topics to ensure diverse content coverage:
1. **Service Specials** (oil change, tire rotation, brake service, AC check)
2. **Inventory Highlights** (new arrivals, featured models, pre-owned specials)
3. **Community Involvement** (local events, sponsorships, charity activities)
4. **Customer Stories** (testimonials, staff spotlights, customer of the month)
5. **Seasonal Tips** (winter prep, summer readiness, holiday travel)

## Neighborhood Rotation
Ensure geographic diversity in content mentions:
- **Carroll (Primary):** 40-50% of posts
- **Spirit Lake/Okoboji (Secondary):** 30-40% of posts  
- **Surrounding Communities:** 20-30% of posts
  - Denison, Sac City, Lake City, Storm Lake, Spencer, Estherville

## Seasonal Content Themes (May-June)
- Road trip preparation service specials
- Summer vehicle readiness (AC check, tire pressure, fluid levels)
- Graduation gift vehicle ideas
- Lake season preparation (towing capacity, roof racks)
- Memorial Day and Independence Day holiday hours/events

## Image Guidelines
- **Always use original photos** - never stock imagery
- **Service photos:** Show actual technicians working on vehicles in our shop
- **Inventory photos:** Feature vehicles on our lot with local landmarks visible
- **Community photos:** Capture real local events, staff participation
- **Customer photos:** With permission, show satisfied customers with their vehicles
- **Composition:** Include dealership signage, local Iowa scenery, or recognizable local landmarks when possible

## CTA Best Practices
- **Service posts:** Use "Call Now" with phone number (712) 522-2526
- **Inventory posts:** Use "Learn More" linking to specific vehicle or inventory page
- **Event posts:** Use "Learn More" linking to event page or "Call Now" for RSVPs
- **Update posts:** Use "Learn More" linking to relevant service specials page

## Voice and Tone
- **Local and neighborly** - "Your neighbors at Motor Inn" not corporate language
- **Trust-focused** - Emphasize 90+ years serving Carroll community
- **Practical and helpful** - Focus on customer benefits, not hard selling
- **Authentic** - Show real people, real situations, real Iowa life
- **Never mention "days on lot"** - violates dealer compliance rules
