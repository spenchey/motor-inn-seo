# GBP Posting Strategy — 2026-07-09
Based on GBP Velocity Audit of 2026-07-09

## Recommended Cadence
- Frequency: 2 core posts per week.
- Best days: Tuesday and Thursday.
- Optional flex slot: Sunday afternoon only when a seasonal or local-use angle is genuinely timely.

## Late-July / Early-August Content Mix
- 35% service and seasonal utility
- 35% used or certified inventory value
- 30% practical FAQ or buyer-guidance content

## Priority Angles
1. Late-summer service checks after road-trip, lake, and highway miles.
2. Family SUV and truck practicality for Carroll, Spirit Lake, and Okoboji drivers.
3. Straight-talk used and certified value for buyers who want fewer surprises.
4. Trade-in and financing clarity ahead of the back-to-school reset.
5. Topics that can also be reused on Facebook and Instagram because Google is surfacing broader profile-freshness signals.

## Competitor Signals To Counter
- Champion Ford still shows a visible owner-post archive with simple vehicle and promo hooks.
- Spirit Lake Ford still shows an active updates module and social-link freshness signals.
- Motor Inn still has no current visible owner post in the accessible live view, so consistency is still the quickest win.

## Location Rotation
- Carroll first
- Spirit Lake or Okoboji second
- Then one surrounding-market mention such as Denison, Spencer, Storm Lake, or Lake City
- Do not repeat the same area in back-to-back posts

## Post Rules
- Keep copy between 100 and 150 words
- Use a real Motor Inn photo every time
- Include one target keyword and one place mention
- End with a clear CTA tied to phone or site
- Never mention days on lot
