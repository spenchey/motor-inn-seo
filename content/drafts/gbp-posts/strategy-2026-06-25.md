# GBP Posting Strategy — 2026-06-25
Based on GBP Velocity Audit of 2026-06-25

## Recommended Cadence
- Frequency: 2 core posts per week.
- Best days: Tuesday and Thursday.
- Optional flex slot: Sunday afternoon only when a holiday, lake-weekend, or clearly timely inventory angle makes it worth using.

## Early July Content Mix
- 40% service and seasonal utility
- 30% inventory or certified-pre-owned value
- 30% local lifestyle, buyer education, or trip-planning relevance

## Priority Angles
1. Road-trip readiness before and just after July 4 travel.
2. Lake-season towing, cargo room, and truck/SUV practicality for Spirit Lake and Okoboji shoppers.
3. Clear used and certified value for buyers tired of vague pricing and fuzzy vehicle history.
4. FAQ-style posts that answer practical local questions in plain language.
5. GBP topics that can be reused on Facebook or Instagram because Google is surfacing stronger cross-channel freshness signals.

## Competitor Signals To Counter
- Spirit Lake Ford is still running clear, dated, savings-led offer content with a direct CTA.
- Champion Ford continues to use the single-model spotlight pattern with very short copy.
- Motor Inn still had no visible current owner post in the accessible live view on June 25, 2026, so the first win is visible rhythm.

## Location Rotation
- Carroll first
- Spirit Lake or Okoboji second
- Then one surrounding-market mention such as Denison, Sac City, Storm Lake, Spencer, or Lake City
- Do not repeat the same area in back-to-back posts

## Post Rules
- Keep copy between 100 and 150 words
- Use a real Motor Inn photo every time
- Include one target keyword and one place mention
- End with a clear CTA tied to phone or site
- Never mention days on lot
