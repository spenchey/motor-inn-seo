### Post 2: What Your Tires Are Telling You At Every Stoplight
**Type:** update
**Publish date:** 2026-10-22
**Target keyword:** tire replacement Carroll IA

**Copy:**
There is a quick tire check you can do at any stoplight: look at the shoulder of your front tires. If the outside edge is wearing faster than the center, your alignment is pulling; if the center is balding while the shoulders hold tread, your pressures have been running high. Both cost you braking distance on ice. Iowa winters do not leave much margin, and neither do Carroll county roads after the grader has been through. Our tire team will measure all four, show you the wear pattern, and give you a straight answer on rotation, pressure, or replacement. Five minutes, no obligation, before the snow flies.
