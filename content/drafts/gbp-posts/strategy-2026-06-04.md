# GBP Posting Strategy — 2026-06-04
Based on GBP Velocity Audit of 2026-06-04

## Recommended Cadence
- Frequency: 2 core posts per week, with a 3rd post when inventory or local timing gives a real reason.
- Best days: Tuesday and Thursday.
- Optional flex slot: Sunday afternoon for lifestyle/inventory content before the Monday search cycle.

## June Content Mix
- 40% service and seasonal utility
- 30% inventory or certified/pre-owned value
- 30% local lifestyle, community, or buyer education

## Priority Angles
1. Summer road-trip prep for Carroll families and commuters
2. Lake-season towing, cargo room, and truck/SUV practicality for Spirit Lake and Okoboji shoppers
3. Used-car value and certified peace of mind while shoppers compare options
4. FAQ-style service and buying posts that answer real local questions in plain English

## Location Rotation
- Carroll first
- Spirit Lake / Okoboji second
- Then one surrounding-market mention: Denison, Sac City, Lake City, Storm Lake, Spencer, or Estherville
- Do not repeat the same area in back-to-back posts

## Post Rules
- Keep copy between 100 and 150 words
- Use a real Motor Inn photo every time
- Include one target keyword and one place mention
- End with a clear CTA tied to phone or site
- Never mention days on lot
