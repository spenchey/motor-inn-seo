### Post 3: Educational - Summer Tire Safety
**Type:** update
**Publish date:** 2026-06-02 (Tuesday)
**Target keyword:** tire service Carroll IA
**Location:** Carroll

**Copy:**
As temperatures rise, so does the importance of proper tire maintenance. Hot Iowa pavement combined with underinflated tires increases blowout risk and reduces fuel economy. For every 10-degree temperature increase, tire pressure can rise by 1-2 psi - meaning that tire filled to 35 psi in cool morning could reach 40+ psi by afternoon heat. We recommend checking tire pressure when tires are cold (before driving or after less than 1 mile), using the PSI listed on your driver's door jamb (not the tire sidewall). Don't forget to inspect tread depth - insert a quarter upside down into the tread; if you can see the top of Washington's head, it's time for new tires. Proper tire maintenance improves safety, fuel efficiency, and handling - especially important for summer road trips to Okoboji or daily commutes around Carroll. Stop by for a free tire pressure check and tread depth evaluation - we'll get you road-trip ready.
