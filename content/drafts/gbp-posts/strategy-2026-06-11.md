Based on GBP Velocity Audit of 2026-06-11

- Optional flex slot: Sunday afternoon when a lake-weekend or holiday-week angle is timely.

## Late June Content Mix
- 40% service and seasonal utility

- 30% local lifestyle, buyer education, or community relevance

4. FAQ-style posts that answer real local questions in plain language
5. Content reuse across GBP and social because profile freshness now extends beyond the listing itself
