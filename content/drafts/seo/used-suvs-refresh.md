## What should I check on a used SUV before I buy?

Ask four things about the exact unit: its mileage and service records, its vehicle history report, how the tires and brakes measure, and the full price in writing. The price shown on our vehicle pages includes the $180 doc fee, so the number you see is the number you start from. Our team answers the rest from the vehicle's own records, and you can verify anything on the vehicle page or in person before you decide.

That short list works at any dealership. If a seller cannot answer the history or the out-the-door price directly, that tells you something too.
