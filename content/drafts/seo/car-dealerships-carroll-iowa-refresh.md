# Car Dealerships in Carroll, Iowa

Carroll, Iowa has several car dealerships within a few miles of each other. Motor Inn Auto Group, at 1526 Le Clark Road, is the local Toyota and Chevrolet store, family-owned since 1937. For a question about any vehicle here, call 712-792-5000.

## What should I compare between Carroll car dealerships?

Four questions tell you most of what you need to know. Does the store have the vehicle you want listed today? Does it service what it sells on the same lot? Will it put a trade figure in writing? Is the full price, including fees, clear before you drive over?

Brand coverage matters too. Motor Inn lists new Toyotas and new Chevrolets, plus used vehicles across brands.

## Is Motor Inn actually in Carroll?

Yes. Motor Inn Auto Group is at 1526 Le Clark Road, Carroll, IA 51401, and [hours and directions](/hours.aspx) are on the site. Drivers come in from Lake City, Denison, Audubon, Storm Lake and Sac City.

## Can I compare new and used in one visit?

Yes, and the listings start from home. The [new inventory](/searchnew.aspx) and the [used inventory](/used-inventory) sit side by side, or by type: [new Toyotas](/searchnew.aspx?Make=Toyota), [new Chevrolets](/searchnew.aspx?Make=Chevrolet), [used trucks](/searchused.aspx?BodyStyle=Truck), [used SUVs](/searchused.aspx?BodyStyle=SUV).

## Will you appraise my trade-in?

Yes. A trade figure on your current vehicle can be part of any visit, and the [trade-in page](/trade.aspx) explains how that works. The [finance page](/finance.aspx) covers the payment side.

## How do I reach the store before I drive over?

Call Motor Inn Auto Group at 712-792-5000 and name the vehicles you want to see. The [contact page](/contactus.aspx) and the [test-drive form](/testdrive.aspx) are on the site as well.
