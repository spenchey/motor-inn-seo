# Car Dealerships in Carroll, Iowa

Carroll, Iowa has several car dealerships within a few miles of each other, and Motor Inn Auto Group at 1526 Le Clark Road is the local Toyota and Chevrolet store, family-owned since 1937. This page helps you compare the Carroll options on the things that change the buying experience: brand coverage, service after the sale, and a straight answer on price and trade.

## What should I compare between Carroll car dealerships?

Compare four things at any Carroll dealership: whether the store has the vehicle you want listed today, whether it services what it sells on the same lot, whether it will put a trade figure in writing, and whether the full price, including fees, is clear before you drive over. Dealerships that answer those four plainly are the short list.

Brand coverage matters too. Motor Inn lists new Toyotas and new Chevrolets alongside used vehicles across brands, so one visit covers more ground than a single-brand stop.

## Is Motor Inn actually in Carroll?

Yes. Motor Inn Auto Group is at 1526 Le Clark Road, Carroll, IA 51401. Check [hours and directions](/hours.aspx) before you come. Drivers come in from Lake City, Denison, Audubon, Storm Lake and Sac City, so if you are making a drive, call 712-792-5000 first and tell us which vehicles you want to see.

## Can I compare new and used in one visit?

Ask and we can walk you through both. Shoppers regularly weigh a new Silverado against a used one, or a new Highlander against a used one, and the listing pages make that easy to start at home: browse the [new inventory](/searchnew.aspx) and the [used inventory](/used-inventory), or go by type - [new Toyotas](/new-toyota), [new Chevrolets](/new-chevrolets.html), [used trucks](/used-trucks), [used SUVs](/used-suvs).

## Will you appraise my trade-in?

You can ask for a trade figure for your current vehicle as part of any visit, and our [trade-in page](/trade.aspx) explains how that works. For payment ranges, the [finance pages](/finance-locations.html) and a call to 712-792-5000 get you numbers to weigh before you decide anything.

## How do I reach the store before I drive over?

Call Motor Inn Auto Group at 712-792-5000, or [send a question](/contactus.aspx) and tell us which vehicles you want to compare. If you already know the one, [schedule a test drive](/testdrive.aspx) before you make the drive.
