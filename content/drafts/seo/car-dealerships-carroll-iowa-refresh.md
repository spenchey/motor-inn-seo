# Car Dealerships in Carroll, Iowa

Carroll, Iowa has several car dealerships within a few miles of each other. Motor Inn Auto Group, at 1526 Le Clark Road, is the local Toyota and Chevrolet store, family-owned since 1937. The differences that matter when you compare: brand coverage, service after the sale, and a straight answer on price and trade.

## What should I compare between Carroll car dealerships?

Compare four things at any Carroll dealership. Does it have the vehicle you want listed today? Does it service what it sells on the same lot? Will it put a trade figure in writing? Is the full price, including fees, clear before you drive over? How a store answers those four tells you most of what you need to know.

Brand coverage matters too. Motor Inn lists new Toyotas and new Chevrolets, plus used vehicles across brands.

## Is Motor Inn actually in Carroll?

Yes. Motor Inn Auto Group is at 1526 Le Clark Road, Carroll, IA 51401. Check [hours and directions](/hours.aspx) before you come. Drivers come in from Lake City, Denison, Audubon, Storm Lake and Sac City. If you are making a drive, call 712-792-5000 first and tell us which vehicles you want to see.

## Can I compare new and used in one visit?

Yes, and you can start from home. Browse the [new inventory](/searchnew.aspx) and the [used inventory](/used-inventory) side by side. Or go by type: [new Toyotas](/searchnew.aspx?Make=Toyota), [new Chevrolets](/searchnew.aspx?Make=Chevrolet), [used trucks](/searchused.aspx?BodyStyle=Truck), [used SUVs](/searchused.aspx?BodyStyle=SUV).

## Will you appraise my trade-in?

Yes. You can ask for a trade figure for your current vehicle as part of any visit. The [trade-in page](/trade.aspx) explains how that works. For payment ranges, start with the [finance page](/finance.aspx), then call 712-792-5000.

## How do I reach the store before I drive over?

Call Motor Inn Auto Group at 712-792-5000. Or [send a question](/contactus.aspx) and tell us which vehicles you want to compare. If you already know the one, [schedule a test drive](/testdrive.aspx) before you make the drive.
