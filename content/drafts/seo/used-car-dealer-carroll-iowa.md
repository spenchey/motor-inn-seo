## How is a dealership different from an independent used lot?

A dealership handles more of the purchase in one place. Here that means you can ask for a written trade figure on your current vehicle, talk payment ranges before you visit, and come back to service and parts departments on the same lot after the sale. An independent lot can sell you a vehicle; most cannot service it.

None of that replaces checking the specific vehicle. The four questions below are the ones to ask anywhere, including here.

## What should I ask a used car dealer before I buy?

Ask for the vehicle history report, the service records for that exact unit, how the tires and brakes measure, and the full price in writing. The price on our vehicle pages includes the $180 doc fee, so the number on the page is the number you start from.

If you are comparing stores, ask each one the same four questions and keep the answers. A straight answer on paper is easy to compare; a vague one tells you something too.
