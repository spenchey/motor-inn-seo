# GBP Photo Upload Plan — 4 Weeks Starting 2026-08-03

## Status
Today's public Google Maps pages did not expose reliable total photo counts, but they did show that competitors with fresher public activity also surfaced stronger CTAs. This plan focuses on weekly consistency and visible proof of inventory, service, and local presence.

## Weekly Target
- `6 photos per week`
- Split: `3 sales/inventory`, `2 service/parts`, `1 people/community`

## Naming Convention
- `motorinn-[category]-[location]-[YYYY-MM-DD].jpg`

Examples:
- `motorinn-showroom-carroll-2026-08-03.jpg`
- `motorinn-service-bay-carroll-2026-08-05.jpg`
- `motorinn-team-carroll-2026-08-08.jpg`

## Geotagging Instructions
- Preserve phone EXIF data when exporting.
- Tag Carroll photos to `1526 Le Clark Road, Carroll, IA 51401`.
- Use Spirit Lake geotags only for photos shot at the Spirit Lake operation.
- Do not use stock images or AI-generated images on GBP.

## Upload Process
1. Shoot on a phone at the actual location.
2. Export as JPG and keep EXIF metadata intact.
3. Rename using the convention above.
4. Upload the same day or within 48 hours.
5. Pair one weekly upload batch with one GBP owner post.

## Do Not Do
- No stock photos.
- No AI-generated images.
- No blurry or overly dark shots.
- No heavy text overlays.
