## Upload Process
1. Photograph each shot with a smartphone (iPhone recommended for geotag accuracy)
2. Export as JPG (not HEIC — rename to .jpg if needed)
3. Rename using the convention: `motorinn-[category]-[location]-[YYYY-MM-DD].jpg`
4. Open GBP in a browser → Dashboard → Photos → Add photos
5. Upload up to 10 per batch maximum per Google's limit
6. **Important:** The first photo you upload becomes your primary image — make it the best shot

## What NOT to Do
- No stock photos (Google can detect and flags them)
- No AI-generated images on GBP
- No dark/blurry/shaky shots — if the phone cam doesn't capture it clearly, re-shoot
- Never include pricing in photo text overlays (GBP auto-generates thumbnails from overlay text)
