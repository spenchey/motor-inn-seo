# Review Response Templates — 2026-08-27
Refreshed for the 08-27 velocity audit finding: **reply quality and reply speed are now the top fix, not review volume.** Live sampling showed generic, typo'd, and 1–2-month-late replies (Deb Lakner, Diane H). Use these to respond within days and always name a service, a vehicle line, or a place. Rules: no two variations start with the same word; never use "We appreciate your feedback" or "We appreciate you very much!"; never mention days on lot; 1–2 sentences, direct and warm — like the owner wrote it at the end of a long day.

**Cadence note:** Reply within days, not months. If a review sits more than a week, prioritize it.

2. **That** Grand Highlander (or [vehicle]) is a fine choice. Thanks for letting Dave and the team help you through it.
3. **Great** to hear the purchase went smoothly. Enjoy the [vehicle], and call us whenever it needs anything down the road.
