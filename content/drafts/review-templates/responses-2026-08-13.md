# Review Response Templates — 2026-08-13
Refreshed weekly cadence. Rules: no two variations start with the same word; never use "We appreciate your feedback."; never mention days on lot; every reply names a service, a vehicle line, or a place. Keep to 1-2 sentences, direct and warm.

## Positive — Purchase (named vehicle)
1. **Congratulations** on the [vehicle] — glad we could get you into something that fits the way you drive. Thanks for trusting Motor Inn.
2. **We** love hearing the purchase went smoothly. Enjoy the [vehicle], and call us whenever you need it serviced.
3. **It** means a lot that you chose Motor Inn for your [vehicle]. Stop by or call if anything comes up down the road.
