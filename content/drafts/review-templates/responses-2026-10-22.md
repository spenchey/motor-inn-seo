# Review Response Drafts — 2026-10-22

### Variation C
That is a review [name] could hang on the shop wall. Carroll county roads give us plenty of chances to earn it back — come see us for the next rotation, wiper check, or anything that hums.

## Voice notes for the GM path

- State the exact star count from the review record. The Joyce Dickinson 4★-replied-as-5★ slip is the one recurring error; the draft header now carries the true value.
