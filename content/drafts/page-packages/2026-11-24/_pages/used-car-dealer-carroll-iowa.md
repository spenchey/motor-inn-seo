## What should I ask a used car dealer before I buy?

Ask for the vehicle history report, the service records for that exact unit, how the tires and brakes measure, and the full price in writing. The price on our vehicle pages includes the $180 doc fee. The number on the page is the number you start from.

If you are comparing stores, ask each one the same four questions and keep the answers. A straight answer on paper is easy to compare.
