## Which used SUV size fits your household?

Match the SUV to what it carries most days. Compact crossovers handle commuting and tight parking. Midsize SUVs add second-row room for kids and gear. Three-row SUVs carry six to eight people, and the largest ones are the ones shoppers look at for towing campers or boats on weekend trips.

Once you know the size, open the listings in that class above and compare the seating, cargo and feature details on each vehicle page.

## What should I check on a used SUV before I buy?

Ask four things about the exact unit: its mileage and service records, its vehicle history report, how the tires and brakes measure, and the full price in writing. The price shown on our vehicle pages includes the $180 doc fee, so the number you see is the number you start from.

That short list works at any dealership. If a seller cannot answer the history or the full price directly, that tells you something too.
