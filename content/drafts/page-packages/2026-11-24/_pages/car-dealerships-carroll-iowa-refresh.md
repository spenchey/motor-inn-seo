# Car Dealerships in Carroll, Iowa

Carroll, Iowa has several car dealerships within a few miles of each other, and Motor Inn Auto Group at 1526 Le Clark Road is the local Toyota and Chevrolet store, family-owned since 1937. This page helps you compare the Carroll options on the things that actually change the buying experience: brand coverage, service after the sale, and a straight answer on price and trade.

## What should I compare between Carroll car dealerships?

Compare four things at any Carroll dealership: whether the store has the vehicle you want in stock today, whether it services what it sells on the same lot, whether it will appraise your trade with real numbers in writing, and whether the full price, including fees, is clear before you drive over. Dealerships that answer those four plainly are the short list.

Brand coverage matters too. Motor Inn carries new Toyotas and new Chevrolets side by side, plus used vehicles across brands, so one visit covers more ground than a single-brand stop.

## Is Motor Inn actually in Carroll?

Yes. Motor Inn Auto Group is at 1526 Le Clark Road, Carroll, IA 51401. Check [hours and directions](/hours.aspx) before you come; drivers come in from Lake City, Denison, Audubon, Storm Lake and Sac City, so if you are making a drive, call ahead and we will have the vehicles you name pulled up.

## Can I compare new and used in one visit?

Yes. Bring your list. We can park a new Silverado next to a used one, or a new Highlander next to a used Highlander, and walk through the differences that matter to you. Start online with the [new inventory](/searchnew.aspx) and the [used inventory](/used-inventory), or browse by type: [new Toyotas](/new-toyota), [new Chevrolets](/new-chevrolets.html), [used trucks](/used-trucks), [used SUVs](/used-suvs).

## Will you appraise my trade-in?

Yes. We inspect your vehicle here and use the same inspection to put a written figure on both the trade and the vehicle you are considering. You can start the [trade-in appraisal](/trade.aspx) online or in person, and our [financing team](/finance-locations.html) can show how the trade changes a monthly payment range.

Call Motor Inn Auto Group at 712-792-5000, or [send a question](/contactus.aspx) and tell us which vehicles you want to compare. If you already know the one, [schedule a test drive](/testdrive.aspx) and we will have it ready when you arrive.
