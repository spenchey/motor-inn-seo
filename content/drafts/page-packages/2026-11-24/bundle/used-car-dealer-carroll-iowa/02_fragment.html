How is a dealership different from an independent used lot?

A dealership answers more of the purchase in one place. Here that means a trade-in appraisal with a written figure, a financing office that can talk payment ranges before you visit, and service and parts departments on the same lot that will know the vehicle after the sale. An independent lot may sell you a vehicle; it usually cannot service it.

None of that replaces checking the specific vehicle. The four questions below are the ones to ask anywhere, including here.

What should I ask a used car dealer before I buy?

Ask for the vehicle history report, the service or reconditioning records for that exact unit, how the tires and brakes measure today, and the full price in writing. The price on our vehicle pages includes the $180 doc fee, so the number on the page is the number you start from.

If you are comparing stores, ask each one the same four questions and keep the answers. A straight answer on paper is easy to compare; a vague one tells you something too.
