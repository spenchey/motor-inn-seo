Car Dealerships in Carroll, Iowa

Carroll, Iowa has several car dealerships within a few miles of each other. Motor Inn Auto Group, at 1526 Le Clark Road, is the local Toyota and Chevrolet store, family-owned since 1937. For a question about any vehicle here, call 712-792-5000.

What should I compare between Carroll car dealerships?

Four questions tell you most of what you need to know. Does the store have the vehicle you want listed today? Does it service what it sells on the same lot? Will it put a trade figure in writing? Is the full price, including fees, clear before you drive over?

Brand coverage matters too. Motor Inn lists new Toyotas and new Chevrolets, plus used vehicles across brands.
