Used Trucks for Towing in Iowa

Start with the trailer weight, not the grille. Then check tow rating, payload, and service history on the exact truck.

A used truck is a good towing pick when the exact truck can handle your load and the paperwork proves it. Newer Silverado 1500 models can tow 7,200 to 13,700 pounds. Newer F-150 models can tow 8,400 to 13,500 pounds. Ram 1500 can tow up to 11,610 pounds. A used truck can fall below those numbers, so check the VIN before you load.

Which used truck should I buy for towing in Iowa?

Buy the truck whose safe tow rating clears your loaded trailer, with payload capacity left over.

Can a used truck tow safely through an Iowa winter?

Yes. Iowa can send ice, snow, and limited visibility. Before a big tow, check the trailer, hitch, safety chains, breakaway wiring, trailer brakes, tires, and service history.

How do I check a used truck before towing?

Match the VIN's towing package and axle configuration. Then inspect the hitch, wiring, lights, controller, tires, brakes, and service history with us.

Should I buy a half-ton or a three-quarter-ton used truck?

Half-ton covers occasional weekend trips. Three-quarter-ton is easier when you tow often or pull heavier loads.
