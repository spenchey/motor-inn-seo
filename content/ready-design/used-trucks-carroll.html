Which used truck fits my work?

Match the truck to the work: light duty for daily driving, heavy duty for regular towing. On a used truck, ask for the undercarriage and service history before you buy.

Towing: what the numbers actually mean

Towing capacity is what the truck pulls. Payload is what the truck carries in the bed. Check the door-jamb payload sticker and the OEM tow chart.
