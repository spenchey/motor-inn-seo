Towing: what do the numbers actually mean?

Towing capacity is what the truck pulls. Payload is what the truck carries — and the two come out of the same budget.

Payload covers passengers, toolbox, fifth-wheel hitch, the dog, everything in the bed. Fill the bed and your remaining towing capacity drops. A camper heavy in the bed plus a boat behind the bumper adds up faster than most people think.

Check the exact rating for the exact truck: year, engine, axle ratio, cab config. The sticker inside the driver's door jamb lists payload. The owner's manual lists towing. OEM spec pages list both by VIN configuration. Our team will walk the numbers with you on any truck we sell — bring your trailer weight and we'll do the math together.
