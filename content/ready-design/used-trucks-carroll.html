Which used truck fits my work?

Match the truck to the job: light duty for daily driving and lighter loads, heavy duty for regular towing. A Silverado 1500 handles a stock trailer or a weekend camper; a Silverado 2500HD or 3500HD pulls heavier equipment. Check the undercarriage and service history on any used truck.
