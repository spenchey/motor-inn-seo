Highlander vs. Grand Highlander

Which is bigger, the Highlander or the Grand Highlander?

The Grand Highlander is bigger — it's a stretched, three-row family hauler with real room behind the third row, while the standard Highlander is the more compact three-row SUV. That size gap is the whole reason Toyota built both.

Shoppers searching "2024 Grand Highlander length" and "curb weight" (per our search data) are asking exactly this. Exact inch-by-inch numbers come straight from Toyota's spec sheets, and we verify each figure before it goes live: toyota.com Highlander specs and toyota.com Grand Highlander specs.

What's the size difference between the Highlander and Grand Highlander?

The Grand Highlander runs on a longer wheelbase and a longer body, which translates into more third-row legroom and more cargo space behind that third row.

The practical difference shows up in daily use. Load a stroller and a cooler behind the third row of a standard Highlander and it's snug. Do the same in a Grand Highlander and there's room to spare. Adults can ride in the Grand Highlander's third row without their knees in the seatback in front. Full dimensions and cargo volumes: toyota.com Grand Highlander specs.

Do both the Highlander and Grand Highlander come as hybrids?

Yes — both offer Toyota's hybrid, and the Grand Highlander adds a hybrid max option for more power.

That means you don't give up fuel economy by sizing up. Check current hybrid lineup details at toyota.com Highlander and toyota.com Grand Highlander.

Which SUV should my family buy, Highlander or Grand Highlander?

If three rows are occasional seats for kids, the standard Highlander is enough SUV. If the third row carries adults every week — carpool, grandparents, sports team — the Grand Highlander is worth the step up in size and price.

Ask yourself one question: how often is the third row actually occupied? A couple times a month? Highlander. Every day? Grand Highlander. The cargo-behind-third-row difference matters just as much for families hauling gear to Storm Lake or Denison weekends.

Both are rated to tow, with the Grand Highlander's maximum generally higher — but exact poundage varies by engine and drivetrain, so we check Toyota's official numbers per configuration.
