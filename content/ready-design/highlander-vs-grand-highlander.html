Which is bigger, the Highlander or the Grand Highlander?

The Grand Highlander is bigger — a stretched three-row family hauler with real room behind the third row. Exact figures come from Toyota's spec sheets.

How much bigger is the Grand Highlander?

Toyota lists the Grand Highlander at 201.4 in. long versus 194.9 in. for Highlander; cargo space maxes at 97.5 cu. ft. versus 84.3 cu. ft.

Do both come as hybrids?

Yes. Toyota offers hybrid versions of both, and the Grand Highlander also has a Hybrid MAX option.
