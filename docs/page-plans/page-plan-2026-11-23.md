## 2. Interpretation: what customers are asking, and where we lose them

1. **"Which Chevy dealer is in Carroll?" is the biggest leak.** Chevy dealer and store-name queries add up to about 13,600 impressions at an average position near 24, with almost no clicks. That includes 6,400+ impressions on "motor inn of carroll l.l.c. near me / chevy / chevrolet". Google doesn't connect the Chevy store's name to any page, and the Chevy site has no dealer-location page. → **New Chevy dealer page on motorinnofcarroll.com.**
2. **"Car dealerships in Carroll" is a ranking-position problem, not a missing-page problem.** About 7,400 impressions sit at positions 5 to 37, and GSC credits /Contactus/Carroll for them. That URL now 301-redirects to /contactus.aspx (checked 2026-11-23), which is its own canonical, so the editable page is /contactus.aspx. Several URLs compete for this intent. → **In-place content on /contactus.aspx**, plus 301s from the two duplicate URLs (decided, section 4).
3. **Chevy model shoppers see us but don't click.** "chevy blazer for sale near me" sits at position 2.0, "chevy suv for sale" at 2.6 and "chevrolet truck for sale" at 4.4, all with **zero clicks**. They land on the group contact page, which doesn't match what they searched for. → **Model and body-style shopping pages on the Chevy site**: SUVs, Blazer, trucks.
4. **The Toyota store is already strong locally.** "toyota carroll iowa", "carroll toyota" and "motor inn toyota of carroll" rank at positions 1.6 to 5.6 with 170 to 255 clicks each, so we don't touch them. The open gap is **statewide and "near me" wording** ("toyota dealers in iowa" family, about 2,800 impressions at positions 3 to 7, low CTR). The two Toyota model packages below target only 784 impressions combined; other Toyota-model 'for sale' queries are listed under gaps. → **One Toyota-in-Iowa page and two paired model pages on the Toyota site.**
5. **Used cars in Carroll are under-ranked.** About 2,600 impressions sit at positions 13 to 26 even though a used-cars page exists. → **Refresh the live /used-cars-carroll-iowa** instead of adding another used URL.
