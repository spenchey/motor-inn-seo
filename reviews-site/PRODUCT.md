# Reviews-page design context

## Register
brand

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
Carroll-area shoppers and service customers comparing feedback or leaving an honest account of a visit. A customer reads on a phone in daylight, wanting legible source links and clear business identity.

## Design choices
Working choices inferred under the user's instruction to work autonomously, not additional user-confirmed brand rules: direct, local, clear. A forest-green introduction and white review directory, system Arial/Helvetica typography, strong text contrast, and horizontally grouped source rows. No imagery or downloadable fonts are needed for this source directory. No outside assets load.

## Conversion and proof
Primary actions: read original platform feedback and leave a review through that platform. Secondary: return to the correct official dealership site. Counts and scores are unknown placeholders, not social proof. Disclose dealer ownership and source verification status.

## Accessibility
Semantic headings, visible focus, skip link, mobile stacking, 44px action targets, no required animation, and useful no-JavaScript fallback. Validate using the supplied QA procedure.
