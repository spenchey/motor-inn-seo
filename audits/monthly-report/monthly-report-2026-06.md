# Motor Inn Auto Group — Monthly SEO Report
## May 2026

---

### Read This First (30-Second Summary)

**Calls from organic:** 11 (+57% vs April)  
**Organic traffic:** 1,945 sessions (+40% vs April)  
**Review velocity:** 213 total reviews across both profiles, still 83% of top local competitor volume  
**Bottom line:** SEO traffic and call volume moved the right way in May, but form leads slipped and local trust data is still too incomplete to manage GBP and citations confidently.

**Industry benchmark check:** Recent dealer benchmarks say the average dealership website converts around 5.7%, top-quartile sites reach 8.5%+, and about 74% of dealership traffic is mobile. Motor Inn matched that mobile mix at 74% and grew organic calls, but the lead form side is still behind where the traffic trend says it should be. Foureyes' latest dealer benchmark work also says 60% of buying leads close within 3 days, so missed calls or weak contact flows cost real revenue fast.

---

### 3 Wins This Month

1. **Organic call volume rose** — Organic `asc_click_to_call` events increased to 11 from 7, a 57% lift month over month. That is the cleanest sign SEO is still driving real buyer intent.
2. **Organic traffic grew faster than the overall site** — Organic sessions reached 1,945, up 40% month over month, while total sessions climbed to 9,619, up 45%.
3. **High-intent pages gained momentum** — Organic pageviews jumped on `/used-inventory` (+67%), `/contactus.aspx` (+218%), `/searchnew.aspx` (+207%), and the homepage (+109%), which means more shoppers are landing on pages close to action.

### 3 Problems That Need Fixing

1. **Organic form submissions fell** — Organic `asc_form_submission` events dropped to 8 from 12, down 33%, even while organic traffic and calls increased. That usually points to either a conversion-flow issue or tracking drift.
2. **GBP structural and citation data is still incomplete** — The latest GBP structural audit and local authority audit both require manual verification, so category, attribute, service, and citation accuracy are not being managed from current data.
3. **GEO readiness is below target** — The latest SEO/GEO health check scored the site 44/100, with `llms.txt` missing, no explicit AI crawler rules, no FAQ schema on key local pages, and a slow homepage load time around 10.4 seconds.

### The One Thing for Next Month

**Fix the lead-capture path on the top organic pages and verify form tracking** — Start with `/used-inventory`, `/contactus.aspx`, and `/searchnew.aspx`. Traffic is arriving, calls are improving, and forms are the weak link. This is the shortest path from higher SEO traffic to more measurable leads.

**GEO readiness:** Crawlability is open, but the highest AI-search blocker is a missing `llms.txt`. AI crawler rules and FAQ/schema coverage are also incomplete.
