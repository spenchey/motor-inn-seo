# Motor Inn Auto Group — Monthly SEO Report
## July 2026

---

### Read This First (30-Second Summary)

**Calls from organic:** 26 (+37% vs June)  
**Organic traffic:** 1,810 sessions (flat vs June)  
**Review velocity:** Flat; 221 visible Google reviews across both Carroll listings, still behind Spirit Lake Ford's 354-review benchmark  
**Bottom line:** July was steadier than June, with more total traffic and more organic calls, but SEO is still underperforming on organic share and form volume because the Carroll local pages are not live and GBP/citation cleanup is still unfinished.

**Industry benchmark check:** The July 2026 benchmark pull says dealer websites typically get about 41% to 46% of traffic from organic search and about 69% of visits from mobile. Motor Inn ran at 18% organic and 73% mobile in July. The same benchmark set also says 92% of dealership domains still fail Google's mobile speed standards, so faster mobile paths and cleaner lead capture are still the shortest path to more revenue.

---

### 3 Wins This Month

1. **Total traffic grew again** — Sessions rose to 9,903 from 9,118, up 9% month over month, and new users rose 10% to 6,219.
2. **Organic calls improved** — Organic `asc_click_to_call` events rose to 26 from 19, up 37%. That is still the clearest proof that SEO traffic can drive real sales conversations.
3. **The next content wave is ready** — The August 1 website audit shipped 8 local page drafts and 3 GEO answer-page drafts, and the August 3 structural audit refreshed the GBP action list instead of leaving the report stuck on stale inputs.

### 3 Problems That Need Fixing

1. **Organic form volume fell hard** — Organic `asc_form_submission` events dropped to 12 from 21, down 43%. Traffic held flat, but fewer organic visitors filled out forms.
2. **Organic share slipped and is still too low** — Organic traffic was 18% of sessions in July, down from 20% in June and still far below the 41% to 46% benchmark range surfaced in the research pull.
3. **The trust layer is still messy** — GBP still showed 0 visible owner posts in the July 16 velocity audit, citation health still sits at 10 inconsistent listings and 8 missing or unclear listings, and `llms.txt` still returns `404`.

### The One Thing for Next Month

**Publish the ready Carroll local pages and link them from the money pages** — Start with the Carroll dealership, Toyota dealer, and used-cars pages, then link them from `/used-inventory`, `/contactus.aspx`, `/new-toyota`, and the homepage. This is the fastest way to raise non-brand organic traffic without waiting on a full site rebuild.

**GEO readiness:** Crawler access passes today: `robots.txt` 200, `sitemap.xml` 200, homepage 200, and a sample VDP resolves to 200 after redirecting to `/used-inventory`. AI bots appear allowed under the wildcard robots rules. FAQ/schema coverage is still incomplete on key local pages, `llms.txt` is still missing, and the highest AI-search blocker is still citation and brand inconsistency across GBP and third-party listings.
