# Motor Inn Auto Group — Monthly SEO Report
## June 2026

---

### Read This First (30-Second Summary)

**Calls from organic:** 19 (+73% vs May)  
**Organic traffic:** 1,817 sessions (-7% vs May)  
**Review velocity:** Flat; 218 visible Google reviews across both Carroll listings, still well behind the strongest local benchmark  
**Bottom line:** SEO still drove stronger lead intent in June even with slightly lower traffic, but Motor Inn is still capped by a paid-heavy channel mix, stale GBP structure work, and citation cleanup that has not been finished.

**Industry benchmark check:** The June 2026 benchmark pull says U.S. automotive sites average about 41% of traffic from organic search and about 69% from mobile, while dealership conversion benchmarks commonly land in the 3% to 5% range overall and about 2% on mobile. Motor Inn ran at 20% organic and 74% mobile in June. The same benchmark set also shows most dealer sites still fail mobile speed standards, so cleaner conversion paths and faster high-intent pages are still the shortest path to revenue.

---

### 3 Wins This Month

1. **Organic calls jumped even with lower traffic** — Organic `asc_click_to_call` events rose to 19 from 11, up 73% month over month. That is the clearest sign June SEO traffic carried stronger buyer intent.
2. **Organic form submissions improved** — Organic `asc_form_submission` events rose to 21 from 8. The store did not get more organic traffic, but the traffic it did get acted more often.
3. **Crawler access is open and the July content pipeline is ready** — `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, the homepage, and a live vehicle detail page all returned `200` on July 3. The July website audit also shipped 8 new local SEO page drafts, including 3 answer-first GEO pages.

### 3 Problems That Need Fixing

1. **Organic share is still too low** — Organic contributed 20% of sessions in June, roughly half the 41% U.S. automotive benchmark surfaced in the benchmark pull. Paid traffic is carrying too much of the mix.
2. **GBP freshness is still weak** — The July 2 velocity audit found 0 current visible owner posts for Motor Inn, while competitors still show fresher profile activity. Review count was flat week over week.
3. **Trust and entity cleanup are unfinished** — The structural GBP audit is stale from April 27, the citation audit still shows fragmented names and phone numbers, and `llms.txt` is still missing even though AI bots appear allowed through the main robots file.

### The One Thing for Next Month

**Raise organic share by tightening the highest-intent local pages first** — Start with `/used-inventory`, `/searchnew.aspx`, `/contactus.aspx`, and the new Carroll landing pages. The immediate goal is to turn the current intent lift into more organic sessions and more calls without leaning harder on paid traffic.

**GEO readiness:** Crawler access passes today. AI bots appear allowed through the wildcard robots rules, but `llms.txt` is missing, FAQ/schema coverage is still incomplete on key local pages, and citation consistency is still the biggest trust blocker.
