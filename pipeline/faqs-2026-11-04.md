<h2>Light-duty vs heavy-duty FAQs</h2>
<details open>
<summary>What is a light-duty truck?</summary>
<p>A light-duty pickup is the everyday truck class, often under about 8,500 lb GVWR. It is easier to drive and park, and it can still handle light hauling and towing.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What is a heavy-duty truck?</summary>
<p>A heavy-duty truck is built for serious towing and worksite payloads. The live Motor Inn article describes higher-output engines, larger bodies, and greater capability than light-duty models.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>How do real-world capability differences compare?</summary>
<p>Motor Inn&rsquo;s article says light-duty trucks commonly tow up to about 12,000 lb and carry 1,500&ndash;3,000 lb. Heavy-duty trucks can exceed 20,000 lb towing and 6,000 lb payload.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Which truck type is better for everyday use?</summary>
<p>For commuting, errands, light trailers, family trips, and easier parking, a light-duty truck is usually easier to live with. Choose heavy-duty if you regularly tow large loads or haul major payloads.</p>
</details>
