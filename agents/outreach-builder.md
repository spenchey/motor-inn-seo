# 13. Mention/outreach shortlist builder (Holmberg step 5)

## Purpose
Find the third-party pages AI answers already cite for our money queries, where competitors
appear and we don't; produce a useful-reason pitch for each.

## Inputs
Research files (AI-citation tables); DataForSEO; Firecrawl; competitor-read artifacts.

## Exact steps
1. From each brain/research/*/research.md, collect cited domains for our target queries.
2. For the top cited pages: scrape and check whether Motor Inn appears.
3. Shortlist = pages where (a) competitors listed, (b) we absent, (c) page ranks or is
   cited in AI answers for our queries.
4. For each: record page URL + audience, WHY Motor Inn belongs (inventory breadth, service
   capability, local facts - only true ones), supporting evidence links, and a 90-word
   pitch draft that leads with value for the editor's readers.
5. Save brain/outreach/<date>-shortlist.md. NEVER send: outreach emails go through the
   approval queue like everything else (one ask per email).
6. Track state per domain in brain/outreach/LEDGER.md (contacted/waiting/placed/no).
