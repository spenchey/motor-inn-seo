# 10x ARTICLE METHOD (2whazzuup) - run for EVERY SEO article, before drafting
Inputs: our domain, competitor URL, target keyword, audience.
1. Reverse-engineer competitor: intent, structure, depth, trust, UX.
2. Find value gaps (not 'longer' - missing).
3. Research + fact-check primary sources only; no fabrication.
4. Strategy: 5-10 concrete improvements, keyword plan, outline, information-gain.
5. Write w/ SEO rules + house voice (see VOICE-DNA.md); avoid AI-tell patterns.
6. Audit: scored table vs competitor across 8 categories; iterate until wins majority.
7. Deliverables: comparison, final HTML (DealerOn format), metadata, internal links
   (run internal-links audit rules), sources.
Non-negotiables: no copying, no invented facts (facts.md gate), no filler, no guarantees.
