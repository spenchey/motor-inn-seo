# 10. Local citations and community authority

## Purpose
Improve accurate local business references through real Carroll-area relationships. This replaces generic link outreach.

## Inputs
Job 4 citation gaps, current official dealership name/address/phone/website per entity, existing membership/sponsorship records, current profile URLs, previous correction log.

## Exact steps
1. Verify NAP and correct Toyota/Chevrolet/group destination against current official pages and owner records. Do not assume a tracking phone on a marketplace is the canonical business number.
2. Inventory Carroll Chamber of Commerce/Carroll Area Development Corporation membership directory (`carrolliowa.com`), Carroll Daily Times Herald / Carroll Times Herald (`carrolltimesherald.com`; older references use `carrollspaper.com`), current local sponsor/partner pages, Iowa Automobile Dealers Association (`iada.com`) and Iowa Independent Automobile Dealers Association (`iowaiada.com`) where actual membership and eligibility fit. These are research targets, not claims of membership or guaranteed listings. Verify the current organization/site before use.
3. Search each source for Motor Inn and the Carroll entity; save exact profile/article URL, date and visible NAP/link. Use active sponsorship records to select school, sports, charity or community-event pages in Carroll, Lake City, Denison, Audubon, Storm Lake and Sac City. Do not invent sponsorships.
4. Classify accurate, outdated, duplicate, not-found-in-search, confirmed-missing or inaccessible. Check links resolve to the right canonical host; do not replace legitimate franchise identities with the group name everywhere.
5. Prioritize identity/URL corrections first, existing legitimate relationship omissions second, editorial story opportunities third. Score each as high/medium/low based on core-market audience and evidence of referral/citation relevance; record unknown ROI honestly.
6. Prepare a local correction packet or story idea with factual evidence, proposed copy, exact destination and owner. A sponsorship expense or membership application requires separate approval. Paid placements must be identified and links appropriately qualified by the publisher; do not buy ranking credit.
7. Skip link exchanges entirely. Reciprocal-link campaigns have low expected ROI for these franchised dealers, dilute local relevance and create avoidable link-spam/reputation risk. No exchange templates, paid-link brokers or mass outreach lists.
8. Save proposals only. Do not email, submit directory edits, purchase membership or contact journalists. After a separately authorized correction, verify the live page and retain a before/after record.
